## Overview

This project investigated trustworthy weakly supervised breast cancer detection in breast ultrasound imaging. It focused on BUSwiNet, a weakly supervised framework using image-level labels to identify breast tumor bounding boxes and classify tumors. The project also examined Bayesian neural networks for quantitative trustworthiness evaluation and supported undergraduate research mentoring in AI and healthcare.
